\section{\headingprofile}
Two to four sentences positioning the candidate for this specific role. State years of
experience, the two or three domains that matter most for the target job, and the
concrete systems or methods the ad asks for. No adjectives that cannot be evidenced
further down.

%----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
%    EXPERIENCE
%----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

\section{\headingexperience}

\begin{joblong}{Role Title — Employer Name}{MM/JJJJ - heute}
\item Achievement or responsibility, phrased in the vocabulary the target ad uses
\item Scope indicator — volume, team size, systems owned, budget where genuinely known
\item Outcome with a number where a real one exists; qualitative where it does not
\item Cross-functional work, named by the function rather than generically
\item Ownership statement matching the candidate's actual level of involvement
\end{joblong}

\begin{joblong}{Role Title — Employer Name}{MM/JJJJ - MM/JJJJ}
\item Achievement or responsibility
\item Scope indicator
\item Outcome
\item Collaboration or process contribution
\end{joblong}

\begin{joblong}{Role Title — Employer Name}{MM/JJJJ - MM/JJJJ}
\item Achievement or responsibility
\item Named systems or tools actually used
\item Outcome or process improvement
\end{joblong}
